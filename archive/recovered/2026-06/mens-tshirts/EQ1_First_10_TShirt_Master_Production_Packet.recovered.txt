Branding Rule
All tees carry centered lowercase 'equilibrium' in Rubik 400, matte tone-on-tone. Logo contrast target: 8-12% on clean fabrics, 12-18% on washed/acid fabrics.
Centered wordmark artwork target: width 90-120 mm on size M; letter height 8-11 mm; center placement approximately 190-210 mm down from high shoulder point.
