## Collection Thesis

A premium female tracksuit collection built from the approved EQ1 swatch library and uploaded tracksuit silhouette references. The collection mixes clean monochrome sets with disciplined contrast blocking, side rails, sleeve bands, and track stripes. The visual language is sporty, feminine, modern, wearable, and commercially clean rather than gym-uniform or performance-only.

## Locked Garment System

Approved top families:

- quarter-zip pullover
- full-zip track jacket
- long-sleeve crewneck top
- relaxed short-sleeve crewneck top

Trousers are mid-rise and comfortable through hip/thigh, then straight or lightly tapered. They must never read as leggings.

Fit is a flattering regular women's fit: clean through shoulder and torso; never oversized, bodycon, cropped, or exaggerated.

## Branding

- Top: **EQ1** on the wearer's **left chest / top-left side**.
- Trouser: **EQ1** on the **front of the wearer's right upper leg / thigh**.
- EQ1 is the only required visible text.
- No slogans, random labels, numbers, patches, or third-party marks.

## Construction Language

Contrast is produced with manufacturable sewn panels, piping, sleeve rails, twin bands, and outer-leg stripes. Panel widths should stay restrained and premium; avoid oversized racing stripes and excessive color blocking.

Other global construction rules:

- clean premium zipper hardware for quarter/full-zip variants
- functional side pockets integrated cleanly
- elasticated waistband with internal/minimal drawcord treatment
- straight trouser hem by default; clean cuff only when explicitly specified
- no decorative zipper charms or unrelated hardware

## Output System

Every design uses the complete EQ1 three-output sequence:

1. **Product:** front + back flat views of the complete same tracksuit in one square image, no model.
2. **Models:** two adult Pakistani female models in the same tracksuit, one front and one back.
3. **Construction:** fabric texture, seams, panel/stripe joins, zipper/neck finish, pockets, waistband, EQ1 application, and trouser hem only.

Final assets: exactly one **1024 × 1024** image per requested output.
