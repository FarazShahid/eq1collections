## Collection Definition

The Line is a ladies-only premium coordinated long-T-shirt collection built around a modern **women's knee-length long T-shirt** in soft premium cotton-polyester T-shirt jersey. It must read as a contemporary long T-shirt, never as a kurta, blouse, woven shirt, kaftan, hoodie, sweatshirt, formal dress, or traditional dress.

The collection is intentionally garment-led and contains **no main artwork**. Product variation comes from color pairing, female-specific shaping, side slits, hem treatments, sleeve proportion, trouser fit, and selected removable self-fabric belt / soft-form variants.

## Approved Palette

- Pistachio Green
- Bubble Pink
- Black
- Microsoft Blue
- Gray

Bottoms use the same five-color palette and may be straight, slim-straight, cigarette, fitted trousers, or tights only where specified.

## Garment / Fit Rules

- female-specific relaxed fit
- softly controlled shoulders
- round crew neck
- clean T-shirt sleeves ending near the elbow
- natural drape
- knee-length body
- relaxed and feminine, never bodycon
- never a boxy men's oversized T-shirt transferred onto a female model
- no denim, cargo pants, skirts, shorts, or formal tailoring

Form variants may use a removable self-fabric belt and a restrained soft A-line / frock-inspired lower fall, but the upper section must remain unmistakably T-shirt construction and the product must stay contemporary rather than traditional.

## Branding

- text: **EQ1** exactly
- placement: wearer's upper-left chest
- width: approximately **2.5 inches**
- typography direction: **Rubik 600 / SemiBold uppercase**
- branding stays small, premium, and consistent
- no additional logos, slogans, illustrations, patterns, floral graphics, decorative typography, or large back prints

## Model Direction

The source locks a **realistic curvy, mid-size hourglass** model baseline with full bust, clearly defined waist, rounded hips, fuller thighs, and naturally feminine proportions. It explicitly excludes slim, petite, straight, narrow-hipped, and athletic/bodybuilder proportions.

Other model rules:

- adult Pakistani female models only
- fair-to-medium Pakistani complexion
- black or very dark-brown hair
- premium Pakistani / Persian-influenced editorial presentation
- contemporary, elegant, non-sexualized styling
- garment must drape naturally rather than stretch tightly over bust/hips

This model direction is collection-specific and should not be generalized to other EQ1 women's collections where later files specify broader body-type diversity.

## Campaign / Background Direction

The Line uses sharp foreground product presentation against strongly blurred **warm-neutral cinematic studio or modern architectural backgrounds**. This differs from Ravaani 220's later Lahore-after-rain campaign direction and should remain collection-specific unless intentionally revised.

## Output System

Each design follows the complete three-image EQ1 sequence:

1. **Product:** front + back flat views of the complete coordinated set, no model.
2. **Models:** two adult Pakistani women wearing the same set, one front and one back.
3. **Construction:** fabric and construction details only; no invented decorative artwork.

All outputs are one square **1024 × 1024** web asset each.

## Relationship to Ravaani 220

The Line and Ravaani 220 share the long-T-shirt product language but are **not the same collection**. The Line contains 20 designs, a five-color palette including Gray as a main garment color, stronger form/soft-flare experimentation, and a collection-specific curvy model baseline. Ravaani 220 is a later 12-design structure with four primary colors and three variants per color. Preserve both as separate source histories rather than merging them.
