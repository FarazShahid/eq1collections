## Collection Definition

Create a ladies-only premium coordinated long-T-shirt collection for a young audience.

**Ravaani** is a modern modestwear collection designed to help women move freely and confidently. Made with substantial **220 GSM Cotton-Polyester Jersey Blend**, each comfortable, practical set provides reliable coverage without compromising contemporary style or everyday mobility.

- Collection size: exactly 12 designs.
- Fabric: substantial soft premium **220 GSM Cotton-Polyester Jersey Blend**.
- Garment: women’s knee-length long T-shirt with coordinated trousers or tights.
- Identity: unmistakably a modern long T-shirt — never a kurta, blouse, woven shirt, kaftan, hoodie, sweatshirt, formal dress, traditional dress, or frock.
- Fit: female-specific relaxed fit, softly controlled shoulders, round crew neck, sleeves near the elbow, natural drape, knee-length body.
- Fit must remain feminine and relaxed, never bodycon and never a boxy men’s oversized T-shirt transferred to a woman.
- Main artwork: none. This is a minimal garment-led collection.
- Differentiate products through color pairing, shaping, side slits, subtle hem treatment, sleeve proportion, trouser fit, and selected removable self-fabric belt variants.

## Construction Rules

- Round crew neck.
- Soft feminine shoulder line.
- Clean T-shirt sleeves ending near the elbow.
- Knee-length long-T-shirt body.
- Natural jersey gravity and smooth drape.
- Straight or subtly shaped body according to design.
- Selected variants may use side slits or subtle hem changes.
- Belt variants use a **removable self-fabric belt** with controlled waist definition.
- Belt variants must still read as T-shirts through the upper body and must not become dresses or traditional frocks.
- Bottoms may be straight, slim-straight, cigarette, fitted trousers, or tights only where specified.
- No denim, cargo pants, skirts, shorts, or formal tailoring.

## Branding Rules

- Use the spelling **EQ1** exactly.
- Place EQ1 on the wearer’s upper-left chest.
- Approximate logo width: **2.5 inches**.
- Typeface direction: **Rubik 600 / SemiBold uppercase**.
- Pistachio and Pink tops: black logo treatment.
- Black and Blue tops: white logo treatment.
- Branding must remain small, restrained, premium, and consistent.
- Never add oversized branding or move the logo away from the wearer’s left chest.

## Artwork Exclusions

Do not add illustrations, slogans, patterns, decorative typography, large back prints, unrelated branding, unrequested logos, text, graphics, pockets, trims, hardware, or decorative construction.

Keep the back clean and unprinted.

- Fair to medium Pakistani complexion.
- Balanced feminine facial features with a soft defined jawline.
- Black or very dark-brown hair.

- Confident editorial catalogue poses with natural movement.
- White or neutral sneakers and minimal accessories only.
