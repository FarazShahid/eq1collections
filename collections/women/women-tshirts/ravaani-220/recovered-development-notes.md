## Three-design system per primary color

For each of the four main colors, create three collection variants:

1. Same-color long T-shirt and trouser
2. Long T-shirt and trouser in contrast colors
3. Contrast long T-shirt and trouser with a waist belt

This creates 4 color families × 3 treatments = 12 designs.
