## Collection Description

**Ravaani** is a modern modestwear collection designed to help women move freely and confidently. Made with substantial **220 GSM jersey**, each comfortable, practical set provides reliable coverage without compromising contemporary style or everyday mobility.

## Product Thesis

Ravaani 220 is a ladies-only, garment-led collection built around a modern knee-length long T-shirt rather than a kurta, blouse, woven shirt, kaftan, hoodie, sweatshirt, formal dress, or traditional dress. The silhouette is relaxed and feminine, with natural drape, dependable coverage, and practical movement for everyday wear.

The collection is deliberately minimal: differentiation comes from color pairing, female-specific shaping, side slits, hem treatment, sleeve proportion, trouser fit, and selected removable self-fabric belt variants rather than decorative artwork.
