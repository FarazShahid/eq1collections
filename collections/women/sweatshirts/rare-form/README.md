## Collection Positioning

**High-end style for a high-end woman.**

Campaign line: **Not for everyone. Never meant to be.**

Rare Form is built around selective confidence, individuality, premium restraint, and unexpected embroidery. It should feel exclusive without becoming loud, costume-like, or overly decorative.

## Locked Product System

- 280 GSM cotton jersey for body and sleeves
- women’s full-sleeve winter sweatshirt
- premium crew neck with rib finish
- rib cuffs and rib hem
- commercial feminine regular-relaxed fit with controlled ease
- clean curved raglan / soft-raglan construction language
- no hood
- no kangaroo pocket
- no random zipper, cargo pocket, strap, belt, or decorative hardware

## Embroidery Master System

Embroidery is the defining decoration and is tightly controlled:

- appears on both sleeves but **only on the lower half from cuff to elbow**
- begins roughly 2–3 cm above the rib cuff
- ends at or just below the elbow line
- maximum height roughly 40–50% of sleeve length
- upper arm, bicep, shoulder, and raglan seam remain clean
- target roughly 45–60% negative space within the embroidery zone
- one hero element appears **once across the garment**, preferably on the wearer’s right outer forearm
- opposite sleeve carries complementary botanicals, not a duplicate hero
- shapes must be machine-embroidery friendly with sufficient line thickness and no fragile micro-detail
- no chest artwork beyond EQ1
- no back artwork

## Per-Design Embroidery Direction

- **Butterfly / Fuchsia Nightwing:** blush / ivory florals, muted olive/sage, restrained warm-gold accents.
- **Swan / Tangerine Swan:** ivory/silver swan, orange/ivory florals, deep olive/green foliage, small gold accents.
- **Archangel Wing / Olive Seraph:** ivory/silver/muted-gold wing form, sparse ivory/sage botanicals; fashion-oriented, not gothic.
- **Hummingbird / Mustard Hummingbird:** stylized deep-teal/cobalt bird with ivory/mustard botanicals; never photographic.
- **Crane / Black Crane Rose:** ivory/silver crane, restrained burgundy rose vine, olive/forest foliage; strong black negative space.
- **Moon Moth / Midnight Moth:** cobalt/turquoise/silver/muted-gold moth with teal/emerald botanicals; robust antenna/wing lines for embroidery.

## Model / Campaign Direction

- fair-to-medium Pakistani skin tones
- black or very dark-brown hair
- varied realistic feminine body types

Campaign environment: contemporary Lahore outdoors after rain with wet reflective pavement, cool blue-grey cinema tone, warm distant traffic/shop accents, strong environmental motion blur, and model/garment kept sharp.

## Output System

Each design uses the complete three-image system:

1. **Product:** front + back flat views of the same sweatshirt, no model.
2. **Models:** two adult Pakistani female models wearing the same sweatshirt, one front and one back / three-quarter back.
3. **Construction:** 280 GSM cotton jersey texture, rib, raglan join, sleeve seam, cuff, hem, topstitching, side shaping, and stitch quality; do not turn it into another artwork board.

All assets are square **1024 × 1024**.
