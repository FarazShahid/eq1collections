## Collection Cut Language
The collection should feel like one design family even when color blocking changes.

Use:
- clean raglan or soft-raglan shoulder construction
- curved / diagonal sleeve-to-body join inspired by the supplied references
- subtle women-specific side shaping

### Contrast-cut rule
The four contrast colorways — **Pink, Orange, Green, Yellow** — use the visible raglan / curved contrast sleeve cut.

### Tonal-cut rule
The **Black and Blue** colorways keep the same underlying raglan / curved construction language but the sleeves remain the same color as the body, so the cut is expressed through seam architecture and stitching rather than color contrast.

# EMBROIDERY MASTER SYSTEM

## Placement — LOCKED
Embroidery appears on **both sleeves**, but it must stay entirely on the **lower half of the arm**.

### Exact boundary
- Begin approximately **2–3 cm above the rib cuff**.
- Grow vertically upward from wrist / cuff area.
- End **at or slightly below the elbow line**.
- Maximum height: approximately **40–50% of total sleeve length**.
- Keep the entire upper arm, bicep, shoulder, and raglan seam visually clean.
- Never run embroidery from shoulder to cuff.
- Never fill the entire sleeve.

## Scale / Density
- Elegant medium-small composition.
- Narrow vertical footprint.
- Premium visual impact without congestion.
- Maintain visible base fabric around every embroidered cluster.
- Target approximately **45–60% negative space** within the lower-sleeve embroidery zone.
- Avoid a continuous floral carpet.
- Avoid fragile floating micro-details.

## Hero-Element Rule
Every design has **ONE distinct hero embroidery element** integrated into its lower-sleeve botanical composition.

Examples used in this collection:
- butterfly
- swan
- archangel wings
- hummingbird
- crane
- moon moth

### Hero-element placement
- The hero element appears **once across the garment**, preferably on the wearer’s right outer forearm.
- The opposite sleeve carries a complementary botanical echo, not a duplicate hero element.
- Keep the hero element readable and premium, but smaller than the full embroidery zone.
- The hero element must remain embroidery-friendly: connected shapes, sufficient line thickness, no hyper-fragile feather or antenna detail.

## Botanical Support
Each design may combine the hero element with:
- flowers
- leaves
- buds
- berry clusters
- restrained curved stems

No chest artwork beyond EQ1.
No back artwork.
