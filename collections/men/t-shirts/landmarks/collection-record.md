- Recovery status: Reconstructed from surviving chat context

## Core collection concept

LANDMARKS is intentionally simple and graphic-led. Each design uses:

- a **city name**
- an **image/illustration of a landmark from that city**
- **EQULIBRIUM** positioned in the center as a recurring collection identifier

The collection should remain clean, accessible, and immediately readable as a city/landmark story rather than becoming overly decorative.

## Model / presentation rule recovered from chat

Men’s collection imagery should **not** use the District M framing/design treatment.
