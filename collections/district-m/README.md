## Recovered Shared Rules

### Audience / fit

Anime and Original Series collections are treated as **unisex**. When model-led imagery is appropriate, female-oriented series may use women while other series can use the model direction best suited to the collection.

### Series banners

When an Anime or Original Animated Series image is functioning as the **series banner**, use the series artwork / visual world itself. Do **not** place the artwork on a T-shirt and do **not** include a human fashion model in the banner unless a collection-specific source explicitly overrides this rule.

### Garment branding

Where EQ1 branding is specified, `EQ1` is written in capitals.

### DTF production safety

For DTF-led garment artwork:

- keep artwork safely within the printable T-shirt area
- maintain proper margins from seams and garment edges
- avoid fragile thin corners
- avoid very thin typography
- avoid tiny unsupported sticker-like details likely to fail in production

### Source priority

These are shared recovered rules. A collection-specific master prompt, design bible, tech pack, or approved production file always overrides this document when there is a conflict.
