# EQ1 × Chainsaw Man

- Source: EQ1 project chat history
- Original design session: 2026-07-14
- Recovery status: Reconstructed from surviving chat context + corroborated historical source assets
- Category: District M → Anime
- Product: Cotton / cotton-polyester T-shirts
- Fit: Simple UK cut; not drop-shoulder
- Print method direction: DTF-safe artwork

## Recovered color direction

The early working palette included:

- Maroon
- Blue / blue shades
- Green
- Cream
- White
- Black for later character variations

## DTF production constraints

Artwork must stay well within the T-shirt print area with proper margins. Avoid fragile DTF elements such as very thin corners, delicate sticker edges, and overly thin typography. Later revisions also requested the DTF artwork to be approximately **15% smaller** than earlier generations.

## Character development recovered from chat

### Himeno

Multiple additional T-shirts were developed, including black, blue shades, and green.

### Makima

Six T-shirt concepts were requested. The creative direction emphasized **Makima’s eyes and power** as the central visual idea.

### Power

The collection then moved to Power-focused designs.

### Denji + Pochita

The direction called for **brush-stroke artwork with a high-energy feel**, followed by five individual T-shirt designs.

## Output / garment rules

- Individual T-shirt visuals were preferred.
- Cotton T-shirts were used for the artwork.
- Garments should use a conventional UK-cut silhouette, not a drop-shoulder silhouette.
