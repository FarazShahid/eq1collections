## Recovered color direction

The early working palette included:

- Maroon
- Blue / blue shades
- Green
- Cream
- White
- Black for later character variations

## DTF production constraints

Artwork must stay well within the T-shirt print area with proper margins. Avoid fragile DTF elements such as very thin corners, delicate sticker edges, and overly thin typography. Later revisions also requested the DTF artwork to be approximately **15% smaller** than earlier generations.
