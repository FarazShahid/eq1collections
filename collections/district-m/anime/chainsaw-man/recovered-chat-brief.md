# EQ1 × Chainsaw Man — Recovered Project Brief

> Recovered from the visible EQ1 project conversation dated 2026-07-14. This file preserves explicit development instructions; it does not invent missing generated outputs.

## Garment / palette direction

- Cotton / cotton-polyester T-shirts were used during development.
- Initial working colors included maroon, blue, green, cream, and white.
- Later character work also used black and multiple blue shades.
- T-shirts are simple UK cut, **not** drop-shoulder.

## DTF production rules

- Artwork must remain well within the T-shirt with proper margin.
- Avoid thin corners / fragile sticker edges.
- Avoid thin text because those details are vulnerable in DTF production.
- Later artwork was requested approximately **15% smaller** than the earlier scale.

## Character development

### Himeno

Additional Himeno designs were requested in black, blue shades, and green.

### Makima

Six Makima T-shirts were requested. The design direction then focused more strongly on **Makima’s eyes and power**.

### Power

Development moved next to Power-led designs.

### Denji + Pochita

Direction: Denji and Pochita with **brush-stroke art** and a **high-energy feel**, followed by five individual T-shirt designs.

## Output rule

Individual T-shirt images were preferred rather than combined collection sheets.
